Góis, between river and mountains

Góis lies between the Serra da Lousã and Serra do Açor ranges and is crossed by the River Ceira.

Choose an experienceAsk for information

Activities connected to Góis

Start with the experience you want to have. Open its activity page for preparation advice and questions about the route.

Water Walking

Rio Ceira · Góis

Discover the River Ceira from its riverbed, among water, rock and riverside vegetation. Water Walking combines walking and swimming, small rapids and natural obstacles. Jumps are optional, with alternative ways to progress.

View activity

Canyoning · Pena

Góis

Discover canyoning at Ribeira da Pena in Góis, among rock and water. The descent combines moving through the stream with abseiling. Jumps and natural slides are optional.

View activity

Understand the setting, choose the route

The river and mountains help you understand the region. The exact location and conditions depend on your chosen activity.

Serra da Lousã

Serra da Lousã brings together mountain landscapes, watercourses and Schist Villages. This is regional context, not a TS Aventura route description.

River Ceira

The Ceira crosses Góis and forms part of the municipality's geographic setting. A river association does not identify an activity's exact location.

Serra do Açor

Serra do Açor frames mountain landscapes, watercourses and settlements such as Piódão. This reference does not confirm activity routes.

Before arranging your journey

Once you have chosen an activity and a possible date, check these details with the team:

  • Where is the meeting point and how do I get there?
  • Where can I park, and is transport needed between the meeting point and the activity?
  • How much travel time should I allow from where I am staying?
  • Are the season and conditions on that date suitable for the route and group?

Do not use the general location of Góis as a meeting point. Ask for the location for your chosen activity and date.
