TS Aventura · Central Portugal

Góis: outdoor adventure shaped by rivers and mountains

Góis is where TS Aventura starts. This guide helps you understand the setting before choosing, while the activity page and the team remain the sources for exact conditions.

01 · Explore the territory

A municipality between river and mountains

Góis lies between the Serra da Lousã and Serra do Açor ranges and is crossed by the River Ceira. Water, relief and settlements shape the setting, but they do not decide which activity is suitable for you.

02 · Explore the territory

Four Schist Villages

Aigra Nova, Aigra Velha, Comareira and Pena are part of the Schist Villages network in the municipality of Góis. They are cultural references, not implied meeting points for an activity.

03 · Explore the territory

Choose by setting, confirm on the activity page

Water, mountain tracks and group challenges require different preparation. Compare the activity pages first, then confirm requirements, meeting details and the conditions that apply to your enquiry.

04 · Explore the territory

Turn interest into a workable plan

Use the local context to ask better questions: who is taking part, what setting appeals to you and what still needs confirmation. Ask the team whenever the answer depends on operational conditions.
