Rooted in Góis since 1999

From Góis, Trans Serrano invites you to discover the region’s rivers, mountains and trails. Local knowledge, preparation for each experience and a direct conversation with those taking part shape the way we work.

Góis is part of the experience

The River Ceira and the Lousã and Açor mountains help explain the landscape around Góis. Water, rock, vegetation and mountain trails give each place its own character and shape the experiences. Getting to know this landscape is part of the adventure.

A distinctive way to go further

There are different ways to discover the region: walking and swimming on a Water Walking outing, following the stream while canyoning, exploring the rivers by canoe or taking a mountain tour. Curiosity takes us out to explore; respect for the place and for those taking part guides how we prepare each experience.

Local knowledge, a straight answer

We share clear information so people know what to expect and what to ask before choosing.
