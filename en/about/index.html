Rooted in Góis since 1999

From Góis, Trans Serrano invites you to discover the region’s rivers, mountains and trails. Local knowledge, preparation for each experience and a direct conversation with those taking part shape the way we work.

From a life choice to a home in Góis

Paulo Silva and Sandra Marques founded Trans Serrano on 16 August 1999 after choosing Góis as a place to live and work. Adventure and outdoor activities were the project's starting point. The Ceira, mountains and villages shaped its first programmes; the offer later grew to serve other audiences and landscapes. The company is based in the Casa de Campo Vale do Ceira building in Góis. Trans Serrano Aventura, Trans Serrano Viagens, the guesthouse and Góis Camping have distinct, complementary roles.

From the Ceira to the mountain villages

We walk through the Ceira, descend rivers by canoe and explore the mountain villages by jeep. Góis is the starting point for work that connects rivers, caves and the Lousã, Açor and Estrela mountains.

Preparing the outing, accompanying the experience

Guides accompany the river routes and drivers guide the jeep tours. Each activity sheet explains the equipment provided, what to bring and the requirements for that route. Canoe and bicycle hire are separate services and do not automatically include a guide.
