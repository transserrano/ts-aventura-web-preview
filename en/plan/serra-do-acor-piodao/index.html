Schist, water and landscape in the Serra do Açor

Piódão, Foz d’Égua and Benfeita reveal different sides of the Serra do Açor. Schist architecture meets valleys, waterways and natural places such as Mata da Margaraça and Fraga da Pena.

Piódão and Foz d’ÉguaBenfeita, woodland and waterPlan for the right place

Piódão and Foz d’Égua

Schist connects the villages’ architecture with the surrounding landscape. Piódão and Foz d’Égua offer starting points for discovering this part of the Serra do Açor, a distinct region from the Serra da Lousã and Serra da Estrela.

Benfeita, woodland and water

Benfeita, Mata da Margaraça and Fraga da Pena add other points of interest. Choose the places you would like to explore: a village, a natural area and a waterway offer different perspectives on the same region.

Plan for the right place

Explore the Açor mountains on foot, by jeep or by van. Compare the Piódão and Benfeita experiences to choose the pace of your visit and the places you would like to discover.

Serra do Açor · Piódão

Old paths connect Piódão and Foz de Égua; Benfeita, Pardieiros and Margaraça Forest offer another view of Açor. Choose walking, jeep or van before shaping your day.
