Good preparation is part of the experience

A river, stream, mountain route or team challenge: preparation starts with the experience you choose. Get to know the terrain and how you will move through it, consider who is taking part and plan what to bring before setting out.

Soprador do Carvalho caving

Equipment provided

  • Guided route, technical suit, wellington boots in sizes 34–46 and a helmet with lighting
  • personal accident insurance, photographs and a shower afterwards.

Before agreeing your programme

Participation and preparationWeather, changes and cancellationSafety and insurance informationEnquiry or booking?

Participation and preparation

Read the selected activity’s requirements, equipment and practical sheet. Guided activities and independent hire are different services. Tell the team about the group and relevant participation needs before agreeing a programme.

Weather, changes and cancellation

River and mountain conditions affect outdoor plans. The dated proposal or booking sets out the applicable change and cancellation terms. Do not assume the same terms apply to every activity or partner.

Safety and insurance information

Use the activity sheet for the admitted safety and equipment information. Ask the team for the current insurance information applicable to the chosen service; policy amounts and exclusions are not reproduced here without current confirmation.

Enquiry or booking?

An enquiry helps prepare a proposal and does not reserve a place. Where a specific FareHarbor link is available, it opens that activity’s external booking page. Check the provider and terms before proceeding.

Website terms

Different activities call for different questions

Water Walking involves walking and swimming; canyoning combines progress along the stream with abseiling. A canoe outing or a mountain tour raises different questions about preparation. When choosing, consider physical condition, contact with water, the terrain and the individual needs of those taking part.

Equipment and kit: start with your chosen experience

Read the included equipment, personal kit and requirements on the page for your chosen experience. Water Walking, Alva Canoeing and Mondego Canoeing already have their own lists; consult the preparation PDF where available as well. The neoprene listed for Water Walking and the Vila Cova do Alva–Côja route is not a shared kit list for every canoe descent. Before setting out, confirm the day's conditions, river flow, meeting point, travel arrangements and any adaptations or group needs with the team.

Conditions can change

On the River Mondego, weather, river flow and safety affect canoeing. The technical team decides whether the outing goes ahead, changes or is cancelled. For other activities, check their specific information.
