Descend the River Teixeira in São João da Serra among rocky cliffs, waterfalls and natural pools. The route combines abseiling, scrambling and aquatic crossings.

Equipment provided: Wetsuit and neoprene socks, helmet, harness and technical canyoning equipment, guides, insurance and photographs; buoyancy aid if needed.

What to bring: Water and a snack or light lunch, swimwear, a change of clothes, flip-flops and a towel. For the route, trainers with thick soles: sandals and neoprene/canoeing shoes are not permitted. Sunscreen and a glasses cord.

Meeting & return: 9 am, at Largo das Carvalhinhas, São João da Serra.
