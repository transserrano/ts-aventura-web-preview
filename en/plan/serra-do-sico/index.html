Reading the limestone landscape of the Serra do Sicó

Limestone shapes the Serra do Sicó. The Buracas do Casmilo, rocky outcrops and field walls reveal the relationship between geology and rural land use.

A different landscape

Limestone outcrops and rock formations give the Serra do Sicó its character. Look at the terrain and field walls as well as choosing a single place to visit.

Buracas do Casmilo

The Buracas do Casmilo are a landmark for exploring this landscape. Visiting a place is not the same as a tour programme: the route and access need to suit the outing and the people taking part.
