How to prepare for a water activity

Water walking, canyoning and canoeing do not call for identical preparation. Start with the right activity page and treat any missing detail as something to confirm, not assume.

Start with the activity, not a generic checklist

First decide whether you are considering water walking, canyoning or canoeing. Each activity page separates facts cleared for use from information that still requires confirmation by the team.

Confirm the equipment for that activity

Use only the list on the chosen activity page and the confirmation you receive. Do not transfer equipment details from one product to another or assume that an item is included.

Share relevant limitations in advance

Where the activity page asks you to, disclose relevant mobility limitations or health conditions. Suitability and final requirements remain subject to the technical team's assessment and confirmation.

Check weather and water conditions

Review official IPMA warnings and follow civil protection guidance. Whether an activity runs may depend on weather, river flow and the safety conditions that apply to the specific product.

Finish the plan with current information

Before you travel, confirm the meeting point, what to bring and anything that remains open. If the page cannot support an answer, send an enquiry rather than filling in the gap yourself.
