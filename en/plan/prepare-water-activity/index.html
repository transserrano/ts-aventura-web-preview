How to prepare for a water activity

Water walking, canyoning and canoeing call for different preparation. Choose your experience and check its activity page for the route, equipment and what to bring.

Start with the activity, not a generic checklist

Choose between water walking, canyoning and canoeing. Visit the activity page to learn about the route, preparation and details to confirm with the team.

Equipment provided and what to bring

Use the list for your chosen activity to distinguish equipment provided from your own kit. Before travelling, ask the team about any item or service that is not described.

Share relevant limitations in advance

Where the activity page asks you to, disclose relevant mobility limitations or health conditions. Suitability and final requirements remain subject to the technical team's assessment and confirmation.

Check weather and water conditions

Review official IPMA warnings and follow civil protection guidance. Whether an activity runs may depend on weather, river flow and the safety conditions that apply to the specific product.

Finish the plan with current information

Before you travel, confirm the meeting point, what to bring and the activity conditions. If a detail is missing, use the team’s contact details to check it before setting out.
