How to prepare for a water activity

Water walking, canyoning and canoeing call for different preparation. The activity pages describe the route and experience; the Mondego page also distinguishes supplied equipment from personal kit.

TS Aventura · Central Portugal

Start with the activity, not a generic checklist

Choose between water walking, canyoning and canoeing. Visit the activity page to learn about the route, preparation and details to confirm with the team.

Prepare for your chosen river experience

Water Walking — River Ceira, Góis

Equipment provided

Neoprene suit and socks, helmet and harness, with technical supervision. The programme provides transport to the start of the route; changing facilities are available at headquarters on return.

What to bring

Bring water, a snack or light lunch, swimwear, a change of clothes, a towel and flip-flops. For moving through the river, prepare trainers with substantial soles. If you wear glasses, bring a retaining strap.

Requirements

Moving through the river calls for agility and physical stamina. Jumps are optional and alternatives are available. Tell the team about relevant limitations before the outing.

Canoeing — River Mondego, Penacova–Coimbra

Equipment provided

Craft, paddle and buoyancy aid.

What to bring

Clothing suitable for getting wet, secure footwear that can get wet, a towel, a change of clothes, water and sun protection.

Requirements

Physical suitability for the route is subject to prior confirmation. Relevant limitations or health conditions must be disclosed before the activity.

The activity is subject to weather, river flow and safety conditions. The final decision to run, change or cancel the activity rests with the technical team.

View the activity and its preparation →

Canoeing — Alva, Vila Cova do Alva–Côja

Equipment provided

Canoeing equipment, neoprene suit, boots and socks.

What to bring

Prepare swimwear and a rash vest, water, a light lunch, warm dry clothes and a towel.

Requirements

Good fitness and agility are needed; previous canoeing experience is recommended. River flow and the group's preparation affect progression.

View the activity and its preparation →

Share relevant limitations in advance

Where the activity page asks you to, disclose relevant mobility limitations or health conditions. Suitability and final requirements remain subject to the technical team's assessment and confirmation.

Check weather and water conditions

Review official IPMA warnings and follow civil protection guidance. Whether an activity runs may depend on weather, river flow and the safety conditions that apply to the specific product.

Finish the plan with current information

Before you travel, confirm the meeting point, what to bring and the activity conditions. If a detail is missing, use the team’s contact details to check it before setting out.

Choose your water experience

Water Walking

Rio Ceira · Góis

Discover the River Ceira from its riverbed, among water, rock and riverside vegetation. Water Walking combines walking and swimming, small rapids and natural obstacles. Jumps are optional, with alternative ways to progress.

View activity

Canyoning · Pena

Góis

Discover canyoning at Ribeira da Pena in Góis, among rock and water. The descent combines moving through the stream with abseiling. Jumps and natural slides are optional.

View activity

Canoeing · Mondego

Penacova–Coimbra

Paddle down the River Mondego and discover the landscape from the water, between Penacova and Coimbra. Your canoe, paddle and buoyancy aid are included.

View activity

Canoeing · Vila Cova–Côja

Rio Alva

Canoe down the River Alva between Vila Cova de Alva and Côja. Weirs and rapids shape this route, whose operation and progression depend on river flow and the day’s assessment.
