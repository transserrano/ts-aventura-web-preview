Frequently asked questions

How do I choose an activity?

Start with the terrain you prefer and the context of those taking part. Then compare the activity pages and talk to us when the choice depends on information that is not yet available.

Can I book online?

Not yet through this website in preparation. Until the booking connection has been validated, use only the information request shown for the activity.

Are the prices and conditions final?

No. Commercial details and participation conditions should only appear after approval in the operational source for each activity.

How do I know which movements are part of the route?

Options and movements depend on the activity and the context that applies to the group. Ask for specific information before deciding.
