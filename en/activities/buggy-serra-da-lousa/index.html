What the confirmation should explain

A guided tour, off-road route, local guide and insurance are documented. The enquiry reply must confirm the applicable package and who is responsible for each part.

Simple preparation, final requirements to confirm

Comfortable clothing, practical footwear and water are supported by the validated information. Other requirements depend on the format and partner conditions.

Personal circumstances and partner validation

Mobility limitations, pregnancy or relevant health conditions should be shared in advance. Participation and final requirements remain subject to technical confirmation and the partner’s conditions.
