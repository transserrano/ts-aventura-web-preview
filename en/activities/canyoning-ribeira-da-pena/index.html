Canyoning in Ribeira da Pena

Follow Ribeira da Pena through rock, water and steep sections, adapting to each change in the terrain.

Read the stream from top to bottom

Canyoning combines movement through water with activity-specific manoeuvres. The applicable route depends on the context and observed conditions.

Height, water and decisions at every stage

The documented route opens with an abseil of about 35 metres before continuing down the stream. Jumps are optional. Group suitability and the applicable conditions are confirmed before the outing.

  1. 01

    Prepare the group

    The team confirms route suitability, conditions and the mobility required before setting out.

  2. 02

    Enter Ribeira da Pena

    The documented route begins with an abseil of about 35 metres and continues along the stream.

  3. 03

    Adapt the progression

    The manoeuvres follow the terrain and jumps are optional, always subject to technical assessment.
