Ribeira da Pena, Góis

The activity takes place at Ribeira da Pena in Góis. The route and meeting point are specific to each outing.

Progression and manoeuvres

Follow Ribeira da Pena, moving through the water and descending by abseil. Rock and water shape the pace of the route. Jumps and natural slides are optional: the technical team chooses manoeuvres and suitable alternatives for the conditions on the day.

Physical readiness and technical assessment

Requires mobility and physical fitness suitable for progressing over uneven terrain, through water and during technical manoeuvres. Injuries, mobility limitations, pregnancy or relevant health conditions must be disclosed in advance. Participation is subject to assessment by the technical team.

Activity photographs

Questions before you decide

Are jumps and natural slides always part of canyoning?

No. They are described as optional. The technical team explains which manoeuvres and alternatives apply to the outing.

Ask about your outing

Include your preferred date and group size when asking about Canyoning in Ribeira da Pena.
