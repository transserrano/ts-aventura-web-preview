    Canyoning in Ribeira da Pena

    Ribeira da Pena · Góis

    Discover canyoning at Ribeira da Pena in Góis, among rock and water. The descent combines moving through the stream with abseiling. Jumps and natural slides are optional.

    Ribeira da Pena, Góis

    Ribeira da Pena flows through a deep, enclosed valley with steep slopes and rocky outcrops. Waterfalls, pools and dense vegetation shape the landscape of this descent in Góis. This setting gives the route its character as it follows the streambed.

    Progression and manoeuvres

    The route can begin with an optional abseil of about 35 metres and continues down the stream, alternating passages through the water and descents using a rope. Jumps and natural slides are optional. The technical team chooses the manoeuvres and alternatives according to the group and conditions on the day.

    Physical readiness and technical assessment

    Requires mobility and physical fitness suitable for progressing over uneven terrain, through water and during technical manoeuvres. Injuries, mobility limitations, pregnancy or relevant health conditions must be disclosed in advance. Participation is subject to assessment by the technical team.

    Activity photographs

    The experience

    Rock, dense vegetation and the Ribeira da Pena stream shape a journey through and alongside the water. Changes in height call for different techniques along the route, rather than a simple walk beside the stream.

    How the outing unfolds

    Entry may include an optional abseil of about 35 metres, chosen by the team according to the group and conditions. Aquatic passages, scrambling and abseils follow, with optional jumps or slides. The descent ends with an exit walk to Ribeira Cimeira.

    Equipment & accompaniment

    Neoprene suit and socks, helmet, harness and technical canyoning equipment. Waterproof packs and containers allow small belongings to be carried; a buoyancy aid is documented where needed.

    What to bring

    Bring water, a snack or light lunch, swimwear, dry clothes, towel, flip-flops and substantial-soled trainers for moving through the water. Prepare a retaining strap for glasses.

    Before you set out

    Agility, stamina and suitable fitness are needed for water, uneven terrain and rope manoeuvres. Tell the team about relevant limitations. The initial abseil, jumps and slides remain optional.

    Meeting & logistics

    Ribeira Cimeira in Góis. The programme arranges transfers between start and finish and an exit walk from the stream.

    Questions before you decide

    Are jumps and natural slides always part of canyoning?

    No. They are described as optional. The technical team explains which manoeuvres and alternatives apply to the outing.

    Ask about your outing

    Include your preferred date and group size when asking about Canyoning in Ribeira da Pena.

    Tell us about your group’s experience in water and with ropes. Ask about route suitability, equipment and the meeting point.
