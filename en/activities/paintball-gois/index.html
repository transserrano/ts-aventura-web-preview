    Where
    Góis
    Experience
    Adventure & teamwork
    Duration
    Around 3 hours, in the morning or afternoon.
    Age and participation
    Ages 10 and over.
    Minimum participants
    Minimum of 8 paying participants.
    Meeting point
    9:30 am or 2:30 pm, at Trans Serrano’s headquarters in Góis.
    Equipment provided
    Paintball equipment and an initial 100 paintballs.

    What you will experience

    An abandoned village in Góis forms the setting. Ruins, passageways and vegetation create cover and tactical choices, in a setting distinct from the Coimbra-region fields.

    Along the route

    Team missions focus on strategy and communication. The format can follow group needs; taking paintball elsewhere requires a separate proposal.

    Included equipment & guiding

    Marker, compressed air, mask, neck protection, camouflage suit and vest. The proposal specifies the paintball allocation and any refills.

    Requirements & preparation

    Requires mobility suitable for moving over uneven terrain. Injuries, mobility limitations, pregnancy or relevant health conditions must be disclosed in advance. Participation is subject to assessment by the technical team.

    Meeting & logistics

    Meet at the Trans Serrano office in Góis. Participants continue in their own vehicles to the field; arrange group transport before the outing.
