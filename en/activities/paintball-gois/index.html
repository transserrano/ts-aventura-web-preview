The plan changes when the game begins

The dynamic comes from the group’s decisions and how each team reads the space, communicates and adapts its strategy to the ground.

Three hours of play, prepared for the group

The documented duration is about three hours and the activity-specific minimum is eight participants. Time, location and format are confirmed through the enquiry.

  1. 01

    Prepare the teams

    The format is organised for the group, with an activity-specific minimum of eight participants.

  2. 02

    Enter the game

    Over about three hours, each team reads the ground, communicates and adapts its strategy.

  3. 03

    Confirm equipment and location

    Time, playing area, consumables and applicable equipment are confirmed before the activity.

Equipment and context to be confirmed

The sources list markers, masks, protective equipment and supervision. The final set, consumables and included services will be confirmed in the proposal.

Ready for uneven ground

Prepare comfortable clothing and footwear with good grip. The final list, including water or a change of clothes, will be confirmed before the activity.

Mobility and technical assessment

Participants need suitable mobility for moving over uneven ground. Injuries, mobility limitations, pregnancy or relevant health conditions should be shared in advance. Participation remains subject to the technical team’s assessment.
