          Where
          Penedos de Góis
          Experience
          Mountain biking
          Programme
          Guided mountain biking in the Penedos de Góis landscape.
          Requirements
          Requires bicycle-handling skills and fitness suitable for the selected route.
          Format
          A guided activity, separate from equipment-only bicycle hire.

          What you will experience

          The mountain-bike route links the Penedos de Góis area with the town, progressing mainly downhill. Ground is generally regular with uneven sections: mountain-bike handling distinguishes this route from independent bike rental.

          Along the route

          The guided route links the Penedos de Góis area with Góis town and runs mainly downhill. Share your mountain-biking experience to plan the programme and access/return arrangements.

          Requirements & preparation

          Physical coordination and some mountain-biking experience are needed to control the bike on descents and uneven sections.

          Meeting & logistics

          The route links Penedos de Góis with Góis town. Arrange the meeting point and access and return transport with the team.
