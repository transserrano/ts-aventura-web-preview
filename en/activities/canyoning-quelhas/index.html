Canyoning · Quelhas

Coentral · Castanheira de Pêra

At Ribeira das Quelhas in Coentral, Castanheira de Pêra, access along the boardwalks precedes a descent into a narrow valley among rock and water.

        Preparation for this activity

          Where
          Coentral · Castanheira de Pêra
          Experience
          Canyoning
          Duration
          Around 5–6 hours.
          Age and participation
          Ages 12 and over.
          Meeting point
          9:30 am, at the entrance to the Coentral Walkways.
          Recommended season
          April–October, subject to river flow and conditions.
          Approach
          The approach includes climbing the walkways before the descent.
          Abseils and progression
          Six abseils approximately 14–20 m high, some optional; the route also includes small jumps and progression along the stream.

          What you will experience

          The Quelhas boardwalks provide an elevated view of the stream before progression along its bed. Abseils, scrambling and short aquatic passages are concentrated in a compact section of Serra da Lousã.

          Along the route

          The programme starts with walking access along the Quelhas boardwalks, then follows the stream through abseils, small jumps, slides and scrambling. Some abseils are optional; the manoeuvres used depend on the outing's technical plan.

          Included equipment & guiding

          • Wetsuit and neoprene socks, helmet, harness and technical canyoning equipment, guides, insurance and photographs
          • buoyancy aid if needed.

          What to bring

          Water and a snack or light lunch, swimwear, a change of clothes, flip-flops and a towel. For the route, trainers with thick soles: sandals and neoprene/canoeing shoes are not permitted. Sunscreen and a glasses cord.

          Requirements & preparation

          Ages 12 and over. The approach requires climbing the entire Ribeira das Quelhas Walkways before entering the stream.

          Meeting & logistics

          9:30 am, at the entrance to the Coentral Walkways.
