    Preparation for this activity

    • Canoeing on the River Mondego
    Where
    Rio Alva · Vila Cova do Alva–Côja
    Experience
    Rivers & water

    What you will experience

    The passages have different forms, from ramps to drops and partially open weirs. Reading the river and handling the boat are central to this experience, distinct from the Fronhas–Moura Morta section.

    Along the route

    Progression follows the route's weirs and rapids; river-flow assessment determines the approach to each passage. Plan arrival in Vila Cova do Alva and the finish in Côja, including driver and return arrangements.

    Along the way

    Included equipment & guiding

    Canoeing equipment, neoprene suit, boots and socks.

    What to bring

    Prepare swimwear and a rash vest, water, a light lunch, warm dry clothes and a towel.

    Requirements & preparation

    Good fitness and agility are needed; previous canoeing experience is recommended. River flow and the group's preparation affect progression.

    Meeting & logistics

    Vila Cova do Alva, Arganil. Arrival and return should be planned for an outing finishing in Côja.

    Questions before you decide

    What should I confirm before planning canoeing on the Alva?

    Check the river stretch, flow, meeting point, equipment and suitability for your group.
