The experience

A different river around every bend

The route brings the group closer to the banks and the changing pace that gives the Alva experience its identity.

Programme

A route shaped by the river flow

The documentary source describes about 9 km between Vila Cova de Alva and Côja, usually from October to May, with a duration of about five hours. Every outing remains dependent on the applicable conditions.

  1. 01

    Confirm the river flow

    The outing depends on River Alva conditions and the current information for your preferred date.

  2. 02

    Follow the Alva

    The documentary source describes about 9 km from Vila Cova de Alva to Côja over approximately five hours.

  3. 03

    Prepare for the right season

    The recommended season is October to May; meeting point, equipment and logistics are confirmed in the enquiry.
