Canyoning · Vessadas

Manhouce · São Pedro do Sul

A descent in Manhouce, São Pedro do Sul, shaped by pools, jumps, slides and abseils. Returning along Ribeira de Manhouce is an important part of the experience.

View dates & bookExplore the route ↓

Dates and booking open on FareHarbor.

Where
Manhouce · São Pedro do Sul
Experience
Canyoning
Duration
Around 3–4 hours.
Age and participation
Ages 12 and over.
Meeting point
9:30 am, in Manhouce, São Pedro do Sul.
Logistics
Start and finish at the meeting point; no transfer is required.

What you will experience

Poço da Barreira marks the start of progression down Ribeira de Vessadas. Drops and pools follow along the descent; the return passes Poço da Silha in a different setting from the downhill section.

Along the route

The programme starts at Poço da Barreira, with a slide or jump option. Abseils and further drops follow, negotiated according to the technical assessment. The return climbs along Ribeira de Manhouce and passes Poço da Silha.

Included equipment & guiding

Wetsuit and neoprene socks, helmet, harness and technical canyoning equipment, guides, insurance and photographs; buoyancy aid if needed.

What to bring

Water and a snack or light lunch, swimwear, a change of clothes, flip-flops and a towel. For the route, trainers with thick soles: sandals and neoprene/canoeing shoes are not permitted. Sunscreen and a glasses cord.

Requirements & preparation

Ages 12 and over. The return includes climbing the Ribeira de Manhouce, the most physically demanding part of the route. Start and finish at the meeting point, with no transfer needed.

Meeting & logistics

9:30 am, in Manhouce, São Pedro do Sul.
