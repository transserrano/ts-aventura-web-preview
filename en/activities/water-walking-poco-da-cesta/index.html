Water Walking · Poço da Cesta

Rio Ceira · Casal Novo

A water-walking route on the River Ceira, from Poço da Mata to Poço da Cesta, following the riverbed among wooded banks and rock formations.

View dates & bookExplore the route ↓

Dates and booking open on FareHarbor.

Where
Rio Ceira · Casal Novo
Experience
Rivers & water

What you will experience

Riverside woodland accompanies a stretch shaped by weirs, rocks and natural pools. Poço da Cesta, with its rocky walls and deep water, gives the finish its character; this is a different route from Water Walking in Góis.

Along the route

The proposed route starts at Quinta da Mata, at Poço da Mata, and continues on foot and by swimming to Poço da Cesta. It involves negotiating weirs and rocks. Jumps are optional; slides depend on river flow and the group's conditions.

Included equipment & guiding

The programme provides for technical supervision, a neoprene suit and socks, helmet and harness. The outing proposal specifies access transport.

What to bring

Bring water, a snack or light lunch, swimwear, a change of clothes, towel, flip-flops and substantial-soled footwear for moving through the water. Use a retaining strap if you bring glasses.

Requirements & preparation

The route calls for agility and stamina for walking and swimming in the river. The proposal should take account of participants' experience and limitations.

Meeting & logistics

Casal Novo, in the Poço da Cesta area. There are no changing facilities at the meeting location; transport to the start forms part of planning the outing.
