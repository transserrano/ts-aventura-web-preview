The experience

In the river, inside the landscape

Moving through the water brings the group closer to the details of the valley and turns the river itself into the route.

Programme

A route prepared in stages

Your enquiry starts with the group context. The relevant conditions are confirmed before the activity, and jumps remain optional throughout the route. Final logistics are shared before the outing.

Questions before choosing

Clear answers, without assumptions

Can I book online?

Not yet through this website in preparation. Until the booking connection has been validated, use only the information request shown for the activity.

Are the jumps compulsory?

No. The jumps are optional. Ask the team how that applies to the route and your group before deciding.

What if conditions prevent the original plan?

The decision and available options depend on the activity and observed conditions. Confirm the procedure with us before assuming a policy or alternative.

What should I bring?

The list depends on the activity and should be confirmed in the information sent for your participation. Do not use another experience as a reference.
