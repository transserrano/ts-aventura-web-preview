Rivers & water

Choose how to explore the river: walking and swimming on a Water Walking route, or paddling a canoe descent.

Compare the routes
  • Water Walking: Góis and Poço da Cesta on the Ceira; Côja on the Alva. Three separate routes.
  • Canoeing: day and night Mondego, two Alva sectors, and routes on the Alvoco, Ceira, Tejo, Vouga and Zêzere.
  • Choose the river and section, then compare duration, effort, return logistics and requirements on the correct page.

Canyoning

Water and ropes open up narrow valleys. Your route determines the approach, descents and return.

Compare the routes
  • Pena: a deep valley in Góis, with an exit walk to Ribeira Cimeira.
  • Quelhas: an approach along the walkways at Coentral.
  • Teixeira, Frades and Vessadas: each river has its own logistics and preparation.
  • Compare rope descents, terrain, total time and preparation; optional jumps do not make routes equivalent.
