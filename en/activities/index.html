Water walking, canyoning and canoeing. 4 activities

Water Walking in Góis

Góis

Discover the River Ceira from its riverbed, among water, rock and riverside vegetation. Water Walking combines walking and swimming, small rapids and natural obstacles. Jumps are optional, with alternative ways to progress.

View activity

Canyoning in Ribeira da Pena

Góis · Ribeira da Pena

Discover canyoning at Ribeira da Pena in Góis, among rock and water. The descent combines moving through the stream with abseiling. Jumps and natural slides are optional.

View activity

Canoeing on the River Mondego

River Mondego

Paddle down the River Mondego and discover the landscape from the water, between Penacova and Coimbra. Your canoe, paddle and buoyancy aid are included.

View activity

Canoeing on the River Alva

River Alva · Arganil

Canoe down the River Alva between Vila Cova de Alva and Côja. Weirs and rapids shape this route, whose operation and progression depend on river flow and the day’s assessment.

Góis, between river and mountains

Góis lies between the Serra da Lousã and Serra do Açor ranges and is crossed by the River Ceira.

Explore the Góis guide →

Choose your terrain

On the River Ceira, Water Walking combines walking and swimming. At Ribeira da Pena, canyoning adds abseiling to the descent. Canoeing follows the Mondego or the Alva stretch between Vila Cova de Alva and Côja. On land, 4x4 tours explore Lousã or Estrela, the buggy tour has its own partner-operated format, and Multi-activities and Paintball offer group challenges.

Walking, ropes, paddling or a team challenge?

Start with how you want to take part. Water Walking follows the Ceira riverbed on foot and by swimming. At Pena, moving along the stream combines with abseiling; on the Mondego and Alva, a canoe follows the river. Mountain tours focus on the journey and landscape, while Paintball and Multi-activities bring the group together around strategy or shared challenges. Open the activities that interest you and compare each experience’s programme, location and preparation.
