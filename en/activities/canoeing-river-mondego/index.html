      Canoeing on the River Mondego

      River · Mondego

      Paddle down the River Mondego and discover the landscape from the water, between Penacova and Coimbra. Your canoe, paddle and buoyancy aid are included.

      The River Mondego by canoe

      The Mondego descent offers another view of the river and its riverside vegetation. Between Penacova and Coimbra, the canoe follows the watercourse and brings participants closer to the river landscape. The route shapes the experience; the meeting point and arrangements for the day are set for the intended outing.

      Conditions on the river

      The canoe is how you move along the route: paddling, steering the craft and following the river are part of the experience. The riverside landscape accompanies the descent as you paddle. The technical team assesses the river and safety conditions to determine the format of the outing.

      Equipment provided

      A canoe, paddle and buoyancy aid are provided. Ask the team about any other equipment or services.

      What to bring

      Bring clothes suitable for getting wet, secure footwear that can get wet, a towel, a change of clothes, water and sun protection.

      Minors and route suitability

      Tell the team in advance if minors are taking part or if anyone has a relevant limitation or health condition. Participation and suitability for the route must be confirmed.

      Activity photographs

      The experience

      Paddling follows a riverside setting where vegetation and birds form part of the surroundings. The small rapids described on the route provide a different rhythm from the more continuous paddling sections.

      How the outing unfolds

      The described route links Carvoeira weir with Palheiros/Zorro River Beach. The descent includes a riverside break for rest and food brought by participants. The river and technical assessment guide progression on the outing.

      Equipment & accompaniment

      Boat, paddle and buoyancy aid.

      What to bring

      Bring clothing and secure footwear that can get wet, a towel, change of clothes, water and sun protection. Prepare food for the riverside break as well.

      Before you set out

      Give advance notice of minors and relevant limitations or health conditions. Suitability for the route should be planned for the group.

      Meeting & logistics

      Start and finish are at different places between Carvoeira and Palheiros/Zorro. The proposal should arrange participant arrival and return.

      Questions before you decide

      Can minors take part in canoeing on the Mondego?

      Participation by minors and the route’s suitability for your group require prior confirmation by the team. Include ages and group composition in your enquiry.

      Ask about your outing

      Include your preferred date and group size when asking about Canoeing on the River Mondego.

      Ask about time on the river, launch and landing points, and the return journey. Let us know if children are joining you.
