Canoeing on the River Mondego

Paddle through the Mondego valley and see its banks and landscape from a quieter, river-level perspective.

Ask about this activity →

What you can plan now

Location
River Mondego, between Penacova and Coimbra
Distance
12 km
Minimum
Minimum 8 participants

Confirm with our team

Price and VAT · meeting point · participation of minors · availability

The route takes shape from the water

The descent replaces the road with the river and invites you to observe the banks from another point of view.

A descent starts before launch

The enquiry starts by confirming the group, route and relevant logistics. The activity depends on weather, river flow and safety conditions; the technical team makes the final decision.

  1. 01

    Confirm the route and group

    Before launch, the team confirms the route, group and relevant logistics.

  2. 02

    Paddle the Mondego

    The 12 km descent replaces the road with the river and opens a different view of the valley and its banks.

  3. 03

    Follow the conditions

    Weather, river flow and safety may change the plan; the technical team makes the final decision.

Equipment provided

The information confirmed by the owner includes the craft, paddle and buoyancy aid. Any other equipment will be confirmed before the activity.

What to bring

Bring clothes suitable for getting wet, secure footwear that can get wet, a towel, a change of clothes, water and sun protection.

Young participants and physical suitability

Participation by young people and physical suitability for the route are subject to prior confirmation. Relevant limitations or health conditions must be disclosed before the activity.

Clear preparation before you set out

We share what is confirmed and identify what still needs checking for your group.

Clear answers, without assumptions

Can I book online?

Not yet through this website in preparation. Until the booking connection has been validated, use only the information request shown for the activity.

What if conditions prevent the original plan?

The decision and available options depend on the activity and observed conditions. Confirm the procedure with us before assuming a policy or alternative.

What should I bring?

The list depends on the activity and should be confirmed in the information sent for your participation. Do not use another experience as a reference.
