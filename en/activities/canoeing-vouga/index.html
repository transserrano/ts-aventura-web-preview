Canoeing — Vouga, Pessegueiro–Sernada

Pessegueiro do Vouga–Sernada

Descend the Vouga between Pessegueiro do Vouga and Sernada, in a setting linked to Santiago Bridge and the former railway now used as a greenway.

Plan this experienceBack to activities →

The experience

Riverside vegetation and railway heritage accompany the route. Rapids depend on river flow and operation of the upstream dam; reduced summer flow can limit progression.

How the outing unfolds

The proposal follows the Pessegueiro–Sernada section. When upstream conditions do not favour the descent, departure can be moved further downstream. The programme should identify the chosen length and access points.

Before you set out

Some previous experience and moderate physical stamina are recommended. Reduced flow and dam operation should be assessed for your preferred date.
