    Preparation for this activity

    • 4x4 Tour in Serra da Lousã
    Where
    Serra da Estrela
    Experience
    Jeep, van & buggy

    What you will experience

    Torre, Poço do Inferno, Covão d’Ametade and the Zêzere valley give the route its mountain character. Linhares da Beira, Folgosinho, Penhas Douradas and Vale do Rossim add contrasting landscapes and heritage.

    Along the route

    A driver-guide adjusts the itinerary to the starting and finishing points, available time and weather.

    Included equipment & guiding

    An all-terrain vehicle tour with a local driver-guide and visits to the stops selected for the itinerary.

    Requirements & preparation

    Mountain conditions can alter the itinerary; snow is not guaranteed.

    Meeting & logistics

    The meeting can be in Seia, Covilhã or Manteigas. Other pickups are agreed in the tour proposal.

    Prepare with the activity sheet

    • Activity & preparation sheet · PDF · 2026

    Questions before you decide

    How are the route and departure point decided?

    Share your date and preferred day format. The proposal sets out the itinerary, departure, any pick-up, duration and applicable conditions.
