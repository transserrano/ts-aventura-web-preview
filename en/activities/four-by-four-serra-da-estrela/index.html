    Serra da Estrela by 4x4

    Serra da Estrela is a mountain massif in central Portugal, shaped by valleys and river sources including those of the Mondego and Alva. The Zêzere glacial valley and historic settlements such as Linhares da Beira and Folgosinho add further perspectives on the mountain landscape and heritage. This is the setting for the jeep tour; the actual route and stops are defined in the itinerary agreed for your group.

    Shape the day

    Start with the places in the mountains you would like to explore and the time available for the day. The journey and stops are organised in the proposed itinerary. Weather conditions form part of preparing the outing and defining its route.

    Check the logistics

    Prepare your enquiry with the preferred date, group size and intended starting point. Add the group’s interests and any mobility needs. The team can clarify the route, stops, vehicle capacity and conditions for your chosen date.

    Details to confirm before you set out

    The route, vehicle setup and inclusions are confirmed for each date and group.

    • People, vehicles and accessShare the group size, mobility and luggage needs. Confirm capacity per vehicle, space and equipment.
    • Duration, departure and routeIndicate your preferred date, available time and starting point. Confirm the itinerary, pick-up and weather conditions.
    • Meals and inclusionsConfirm what is included in the proposal, including whether a meal is provided.
    • Price and unitRequest the total with its unit stated. A price per jeep or vehicle is not automatically a per-person price.
