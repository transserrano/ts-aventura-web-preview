Soprador do Carvalho caving

Algarinho · Penela

Underground river and geological heritage in the Dueça system.

View dates & bookExplore the route ↓

Dates and booking open on FareHarbor.

Where
Algarinho · Penela
Experience
Adventure & teamwork
Duration
Time inside the cave: VC up to 2½ h; VA up to 3½ h; VOL up to 5½ h.
Vehicle capacity
VC and VA: up to 8 participants; VOL: 2–6 participants.
Age and participation
VOL: minimum age 16; this is not a universal rule for the other variants.
Equipment provided
Suit, wellington boots, helmet with lighting and shower facilities.
Meeting point
CISED, Algarinho, Ferrarias, Penela.
Inside the cave
The average temperature inside the cave is around 16 °C.

What you will experience

The visit starts at CISED in Algarinho, Penela, to explore Soprador do Carvalho Cave. Progression follows an underground river through confined spaces, obstacles and crawling sections: water and mud are part of the experience.

Along the route

The Short Visit introduces underground exploration. The Extended Visit goes further into the cave, while Exploration «O Outro Lado — Para Lá do Olhar» is more technically demanding. Select a variant around age and preparation, by advance arrangement.

Along the way

Included equipment & guiding

Guided route, technical suit, wellington boots in sizes 34–46 and a helmet with lighting; personal accident insurance, photographs and a shower afterwards.

What to bring

Comfortable clothing that may get stained, socks, a full change of clothes and a bag for dirty clothing; towel, flip-flops and toiletries for the shower. Children below shoe size 34 bring their own boots, wellingtons or closed shoes.

Requirements & preparation

Progress involves contact with water, confined spaces, small obstacles and crawling sections. Avoid valuables and protect any camera equipment from water and mud.

Meeting & logistics

CISED, Algarinho, Ferrarias, Penela.
