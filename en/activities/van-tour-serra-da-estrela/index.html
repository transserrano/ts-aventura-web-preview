          Where
          Serra da Estrela
          Experience
          Jeep, van & buggy
          Duration
          Half day (3 h) or full day (6 h).
          Vehicle capacity
          Up to 8 participants per van.
          Meeting point
          9:30 am, in Seia, Covilhã or Manteigas.

          What you will experience

          Poço do Inferno, the Zêzere Glacial Valley, Covão d’Ametade and Torre shape the itinerary. Folgosinho, Covão da Ponte and the Mondego valley add heritage; a traditional burel factory is an optional visit.

          Along the route

          Half-day or full-day routes adapt to starting and finishing points, weather and available time. The van allows stops and short walks at the places selected for your itinerary.

          Along the way

          Photographs of the region from the Trans Serrano archive; stops depend on the chosen programme.

          Included equipment & guiding

          • Guided van tour, private route and visits to the mountain villages and viewpoints in the programme
          • local guide, personal accident insurance and photographs.

          What to bring

          • Comfortable clothing for mountain conditions, practical footwear, water and sunscreen
          • a light jacket in winter.

          Requirements & preparation

          The itinerary is adjusted to the start and finish points, available time and weather conditions.

          Meeting & logistics

          9:30 am, in Seia, Covilhã or Manteigas.
