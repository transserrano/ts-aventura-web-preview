The group first, the combination afterwards

The people, context and what they want from the experience guide a proposal that can combine different challenges and forms of cooperation.

A programme shaped around the group

The documented duration is three to six hours and the activity-specific minimum is ten participants. The actual combination remains subject to the confirmed proposal.

  1. 01

    Define the purpose

    The group profile and what people want from the experience guide the proposal.

  2. 02

    Combine the challenges

    An aerial slide, abseiling and climbing may form part of a three-to-six-hour programme, subject to confirmation.

  3. 03

    Set the pace

    The final combination, location and preparation are tailored to the group, with an activity-specific minimum of ten participants.

What may be part of the programme

Aerial slide, abseiling and climbing are candidate modules in the current sources. The selection, supervision and included services will be set out in the enquiry reply.

Preparing for a variable programme

The list depends on the selected activities, location and conditions. The team will confirm the clothing, footwear, water and protection that apply.

Purpose, pace and participation

Share the group’s purpose, experience and relevant needs. This helps adjust the pace without promising a configuration before technical validation.

Clear answers, without assumptions

Can I book online?

Not yet through this website in preparation. Until the booking connection has been validated, use only the information request shown for the activity.

How does a group proposal begin?

It starts with the purpose, group profile, possible dates and specific needs. The activity and organisation are shaped from that context.

What if conditions prevent the original plan?

The decision and available options depend on the activity and observed conditions. Confirm the procedure with us before assuming a policy or alternative.

What should I bring?

The list depends on the activity and should be confirmed in the information sent for your participation. Do not use another experience as a reference.
