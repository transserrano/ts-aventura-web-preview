        Preparation for this activity

          Where
          Vale Glaciar do Zêzere
          Experience
          Guided walks
          Route distance
          Around 10–18 km; circular or linear variants.
          Difficulty
          Moderate difficulty.
          Recommended season
          Spring, summer and autumn, subject to mountain conditions.
          Format
          The route is chosen from Glacial Valley variants, according to the programme.

          What you will experience

          The route follows the Zêzere and the glacial valley’s landforms. The circular version climbs from Manteigas to Covão d’Ametade and returns through Candeeira Valley; the shorter linear option follows only the riverside valley.

          Along the route

          Choose the longer circular hike or shorter linear route, both with moderate slopes. For the linear option, the endpoint and return arrangements are agreed when planning the programme.

          Included equipment & guiding

          Route accompanied by a local guide, visits within the selected hike, personal accident insurance and photographs.

          What to bring

          Water and a light packed lunch, light or thermal clothing for the season, a waterproof or windproof layer, comfortable hiking boots, a hat and trousers that allow freedom of movement.

          Requirements & preparation

          The variant is selected according to interests, fitness and the day’s conditions; routes range from 10 to 18 km.

          Meeting & logistics

          The circular option meets in Manteigas. The linear option has a meeting arranged around the selected route and return.
