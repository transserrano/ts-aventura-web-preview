      Preparation for this activity

      • 4x4 Tour in Serra da Estrela
      Where
      Serra da Lousã
      Experience
      Jeep, van & buggy

      What you will experience

      A driver-guide takes the group along dirt roads, chestnut groves and woodland, stopping in villages and at viewpoints. Trevim connects the mountain views with Santo António da Neve and its historic Royal Ice Houses.

      Along the route

      Choose a half-day or full-day itinerary. Villages may include Gondramaz, Aigra Nova, Aigra Velha, Candal, Cerdeira or Talasnal, selected around the starting point and conditions.

      Included equipment & guiding

      An all-terrain vehicle tour with a local driver-guide and visits to the stops selected for the itinerary.

      What to bring

      Comfortable clothing, practical footwear, water, sun protection and a warm layer.

      Requirements & preparation

      Tell us about the group and mobility needs so village stops can be planned.

      Meeting & logistics

      Lousã or Góis, depending on the itinerary; the exact point is agreed when planning the tour.
