Details to confirm before you set out

The route, vehicle setup and inclusions are confirmed for each date and group.

  • People and vehiclesShare the group size; confirm capacity per vehicle and whether more than one vehicle is needed.
  • Duration and day formatSay whether you have a half-day, full-day or another format in mind; confirm the available duration.
  • Departure and pick-upConfirm the departure point and whether pick-up is possible for your group.
  • Route and weatherAsk which route and weather conditions apply to your date.
  • Accessibility, luggage and equipmentShare mobility and luggage needs; confirm suitability, space and equipment.
  • Meals and inclusionsAsk whether lunch or another meal is part of the proposal, and what else is included.
  • Price and unitRequest the total with its unit stated. A price per jeep or vehicle is not automatically a per-person price.

Questions before you decide

Is the 4x4 tour price per person or per vehicle?

Ask for a quotation that clearly states the pricing unit. A price per jeep or vehicle should not automatically be divided by the number of participants; confirm capacity and the vehicles needed as well.
