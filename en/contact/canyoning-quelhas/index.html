What to include

Tell us which activity interests you, your preferred date and how many people are coming. For a school, group of friends or company team, add the group’s profile and what you would like to do during the day. These details help us focus the conversation on the experience.

Already have a booking?

If you already have a booking, use the contact channel shown in your confirmation and quote only the reference needed to identify it. Do not include payment details or sensitive information.
