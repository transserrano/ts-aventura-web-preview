A day to experience together

Rivers, mountains and team challenges offer different ways to spend a day together. Water Walking, canoeing, 4x4 tours and team activities are starting points for choosing the experience your group would like to share.

Activities to explore as a group

These suggestions are a starting point and do not confirm suitability, capacity or availability. Those details depend on the activity and date.

Water Walking

Rio Ceira · Góis

Discover the River Ceira from its riverbed, among water, rock and riverside vegetation. Water Walking combines walking and swimming, small rapids and natural obstacles. Jumps are optional, with alternative ways to progress.

View activity

Canyoning · Pena

Góis

Discover canyoning at Ribeira da Pena in Góis, among rock and water. The descent combines moving through the stream with abseiling. Jumps and natural slides are optional.

View activity

Canoeing · Mondego

Penacova–Coimbra

Paddle down the River Mondego and discover the landscape from the water, between Penacova and Coimbra. Your canoe, paddle and buoyancy aid are included.

View activity

Lousã Mountains 4x4 tour

Aldeias do Xisto

Discover the landscapes of Serra da Lousã and the Schist Villages area on a 4x4 tour. The route combines the journey through the mountains with the places visited and stops arranged for the programme.

View activity

Multi-activity adventure

Cabril do Ceira · Góis

Bring your group together for an adventure programme that can combine ziplining, abseiling and climbing. The sequence of challenges, pace and participants’ experience help shape Multi-activities.

View activity

Paintball · Abandoned Village

Góis

Strategy, movement and team action in a paintball game in Góis. Read the terrain, communicate with teammates and adapt your decisions as the game develops.

View activity

Start with the kind of day you want

Choose water, mountains or a shared challenge, then compare activities that may suit your group.

Start with the people and their interests

Start with your preferred date, group size, who is coming and the time available. Add your preferences, mobility needs and any travel or arrival requirements. These details help shape a conversation about the day's format and organisation without assuming a booking.

Compare movement and participation

On a Water Walking outing, the route follows the bed of the River Ceira; by canoe, the Mondego offers another perspective on the river. At Pena, moving along the stream combines with abseiling. Jeep tours explore mountain landscapes, while team challenges bring together movement and shared decisions. Compare the terrain, ways to take part and preparation for each activity before choosing.

Bring a preference to the conversation

Share an approximate group size, possible dates and activities that interest you. The team can clarify the format and conditions before any booking.

What helps shape a proposal

  • Approximate number of participants and group make-up
  • Possible dates or preferred period
  • Activities and experience styles of interest
  • Mobility, access or other relevant needs
  • Transport, meeting point, changing facilities and meals: note what needs clarification

Discuss these details with the team before committing to a date or activity.

Basic Pack

Choose a river activity or Aigra Nova's local traditions, with a simple place to stay.

This format centres on a choice between Water Walking in Góis and Aigra Nova's bread-and-cheese workshop. The focus is your chosen experience and a straightforward stay in the area.

Select the experience and share your group and dates. The proposal identifies the accommodation, nights and dinner arrangements.

Standard Pack

Premium Pack
