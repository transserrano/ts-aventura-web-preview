A team day can begin in the mountain landscape or with a shared challenge. Explore 4x4 tours, paintball and multi-activities, then choose around the purpose of the day, the people taking part and the time available.

Activities to explore with your team

These activities are starting points and do not confirm capacity, availability or services. Those details are set out in the proposal for your group.

Lousã Mountains 4x4 tour

Aldeias do Xisto

Discover the landscapes of Serra da Lousã and the Schist Villages area on a 4x4 tour. The route combines the journey through the mountains with the places visited and stops arranged for the programme.

Multi-activity adventure

Cabril do Ceira · Góis

Bring your group together for an adventure programme that can combine ziplining, abseiling and climbing. The sequence of challenges, pace and participants’ experience help shape Multi-activities.

Paintball · Abandoned Village

Góis

Strategy, movement and team action in a paintball game in Góis. Read the terrain, communicate with teammates and adapt your decisions as the game develops.

Choose the objective before the format

Start with the team’s objective, then compare a mountain tour and shared challenges. The format depends on the group proposal.

Set the purpose of the gathering

Prepare your enquiry with the purpose of the day, group size, possible dates and time available. Add the team profile, mobility needs and any transport or organisation requirements. This context helps clarify the activities and applicable conditions before agreeing on a programme.

Choose how the team will take part

On a 4x4 tour, the landscape and route set the scene for the day. In paintball, movement across the terrain accompanies team strategy and decisions; multi-activities can combine different challenges. Compare the scenery, movement and ways to take part to choose the kind of day that makes sense for your team.

Plan around people, time and logistics

Tell us how many people may take part, a possible date and which activities interest you. The team can clarify the format and applicable conditions.

Information to prepare a team proposal

  • Team objective and preferred experience format
  • Approximate participant numbers and possible dates
  • Preferred way to take part: a mountain tour, team strategy or varied challenges
  • Accessibility, mobility and other relevant needs
  • Transport, starting point, meals and breaks to clarify
  • Capacity, customisation, price and terms subject to proposal and confirmation

Discuss these details with the team before committing to a date or activity.

Team-building programmes

Orienteering, multi-activity challenges and leadership games.

Choose map-and-compass orienteering, a station-based Challenger or motivation and leadership games. Messages, rope maze, team skis and tube games create communication and cooperation challenges.
