Learning in motion, beyond the classroom

Learning also happens among rivers and mountains. A day outdoors can bring a class closer to nature and teamwork: choose an experience around the visit's aims and the pupils who will take part.

Prepare a school enquiryExplore the activities →

Activities for the school to consider

These suggestions do not confirm suitability for a class. Route, supervision, capacity and terms should be checked for the visit.

Water Walking in Góis

Góis

Discover the River Ceira from its riverbed, among water, rock and riverside vegetation. Water Walking combines walking and swimming, small rapids and natural obstacles. Jumps are optional, with alternative ways to progress.

View activity

Canoeing on the River Mondego

River Mondego

Paddle down the River Mondego and discover the landscape from the water, between Penacova and Coimbra. Your canoe, paddle and buoyancy aid are included.

Multi-activity adventure

Góis

Bring your group together for an adventure programme that can combine ziplining, abseiling and climbing. The sequence of challenges, pace and participants’ experience help shape Multi-activities.

Water, canoeing and shared challenges

Water Walking, canoeing and multi-activities offer different ways to explore the terrain and take part as a group. Compare what each experience involves and the practical information on its activity page. Participation by minors and suitability for the class require prior confirmation.

Plan the visit

Gather the school year or age group, the number of pupils and accompanying adults, possible dates, time available and the aims of the visit. Include mobility needs and travel arrangements. These details give the school and the team a starting point for clarifying preparation, supervision and responsibilities.

Useful information for the school to gather

  • Visit objectives and pupils’ age range
  • Approximate class size and accompanying adults
  • Possible dates and intended duration, to be confirmed
  • Mobility, support needs and information relevant to safety
  • Permissions or documents required by the school; included equipment and what to bring, to be confirmed
  • School pricing and terms: ask for confirmation rather than assuming a special rate

Discuss these details with the team before committing to a date or activity.
