Questions before you set out

Choosing an activity raises questions. These answers help you take the next step without turning uncertainty into a promise.

8 useful questions

Find a first answer, then talk to us when the choice depends on the group, date or conditions.

How do I choose an activity?

Start with the terrain you prefer and the context of those taking part. Then compare the activity pages and talk to us when the choice depends on information that is not yet available.

Can I book online?

Not yet through this website in preparation. Until the booking connection has been validated, use only the information request shown for the activity.

Are the prices and conditions final?

No. Commercial details and participation conditions should only appear after approval in the operational source for each activity.

How do I know which movements are part of the route?

Options and movements depend on the activity and the context that applies to the group. Ask for specific information before deciding.

How does a group proposal begin?

It starts with the purpose, group profile, possible dates and specific needs. The activity and organisation are shaped from that context.

What information should a school prepare?

Include the purpose of the visit, group profile, accompanying adults, possible dates and specific needs. Applicable documentation will be addressed before commitment.

What if conditions prevent the original plan?

The decision and available options depend on the activity and observed conditions. Confirm the procedure with us before assuming a policy or alternative.

What should I bring?

The list depends on the activity and should be confirmed in the information sent for your participation. Do not use another experience as a reference.
