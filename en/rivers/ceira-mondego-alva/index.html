Ceira, Mondego and Alva: three rivers, different settings

These river names are linked to different TS Aventura experiences. This editorial map adds geographical context and directs you to the right activity page without repeating operational details.

Ceira: the river through Góis

The River Ceira crosses Góis and is part of the municipality's local setting. Water Walking in Góis and Canyoning at Ribeira da Pena keep separate pages because a river, a stream and an activity are not interchangeable.

Mondego: from Serra da Estrela to the Atlantic

The Mondego rises in Serra da Estrela and reaches the Atlantic at Figueira da Foz. The Mondego Canoeing page holds the product facts; this guide does not define a route, river flow or availability.

Alva: a tributary of the Mondego

The Alva rises in Serra da Estrela and meets the Mondego at Porto da Raiva. For an activity on the river, use the Alva Canoeing page and ask the team to confirm current conditions.
